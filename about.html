A lot of wedding films look the same: long, slow shots with instrumental music playing over everything. Beautiful, sure—but where are the people? Where's the laughter? Where are all the voices a couple would want to hear again someday?

Here's what we think is missing.

Audio.

Real, unfiltered, genuine audio.

So we edit differently. We build every film around the voices of family and friends — their interviews, their toasts, their stories about the couple, their love.  And suddenly, the film has a heartbeat.
