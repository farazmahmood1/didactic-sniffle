The Process

how to book your wedding edit

01./

You reach out

Fill out our contact form or send us a message. Tell us about your day and your footage—the date, the venue, who shot it, the vibe. We'll send you our pricing and packages within 24 hours, because we believe in being totally transparent from the start. No surprises, no hidden costs.

02./

We hop on a call

If the pricing works for you, we'll schedule a consultation call (usually within 3-5 days). This is where we get to know each other. We want to hear about your wedding film vision, the style you're going for, and what moments matter most to you. We'll also go through all the packages, look at what footage you have, answer your questions, and make sure we're the right fit.

03./

You secure your editing spot

Love what you hear? We'll send over a contract and invoice. Once that's signed and your deposit is in, you're officially on our editing calendar. We'll send you a welcome email with next steps and what to expect as your footage heads our way.

04./

We plan together

Right after you book, we'll send you a secure link to upload your footage, plus a questionnaire. This is where you tell us about the special moments you don't want us to miss, like your dad seeing you for the first time, that inside joke with your best friend. We also hop on a follow-up call to go over song picks and style notes and make sure we're all on the same page.

05./

We edit your film

This is where the magic happens. We dig into every clip, ready to find the best of it. We'll sync your audio, pull out the interviews and toasts, line up all the candid moments, and make sure the real stuff shines. The laughter, the tears, the audio that makes your film feel like home. We stay connected with you throughout—there when you have a question, giving you space to let us create.

06./

We deliver your film

Once your footage arrives, Mason edits your film with all the care it deserves. Our turnaround time is 3-4 weeks, with a round of revisions included. We know that feels like forever when you're excited to relive your day, but we promise it's worth the wait. When it's ready, we'll send you a digital link to download and share.
