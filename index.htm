our sweet spots

Coastal weddings & church ceremonies– these are our sweet spots

There's something magical about saying your vows with your toes in the sand and the Gulf breeze in your hair. We're Southwest Florida locals through and through, so we know how to bring out the golden-hour glow, sun-warmed color, and soft coastal feel in every frame. Whether it’s bayside, beachfront, or out in the countryside, we'll edit your footage so it looks and feels like the day — and we’re here for the vibe.
