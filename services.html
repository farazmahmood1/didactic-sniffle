Our Process

Simple, clear and stress-free.

You reach out

Share your date, the style you love and roughly how much footage there is. We reply with a clear quote, usually within 24 hours.

You send the footage

Upload via Google Drive, Dropbox or any link you prefer. We confirm everything arrived safely and is playable.

We build the story

We sync cameras and audio, shape the story, add music, grade the colour and mix the sound.

You review & request changes

Watch the first cut and leave time-stamped notes. We refine until it is right.

We deliver

You receive your final film in the formats you need, plus social cuts if included.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I need to send you?

Your raw video files from all cameras, audio recordings from any recorders or lapel mics, and a short note about the style, music and key moments you want. If you have reference films you love, send those too.

How long does an edit take?

It depends on the length of the film and how much footage there is. We confirm a delivery date in your quote, and rush options are available for teasers and highlight films.

Can you match the style of films I already love?

Yes. Share a few reference links and we will match the pacing, colour and mood as closely as we can.

How many revisions are included?

Revisions are included so the film feels right. We will agree the exact number in your quote.

Is my footage safe with you?

Yes. Footage is used only for your project, stored securely and never shared or published without your permission.

Do you work with wedding videographers?

Absolutely. Many of our clients are videographers who outsource editing. We can edit in your style and keep everything white-label.

What formats do I get back?

Standard delivery is 4K or 1080p H.264/H.265. We can also provide ProRes masters, vertical social versions and captions on request.
